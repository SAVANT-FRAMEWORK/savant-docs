    What PRIME Is¶

    PRIME is the constitutional control plane of the SAVANT corpus — not a general assistant, but a routing and governance kernel that:

    1. Classifies each objective epigenetically (five dimensions: abstraction layer, domain, consequence class, epistemic condition of inputs, operator state).
    2. Routes it to the correct specialist layer of the corpus — KEY 1–50 (ten tiers), P-051–P-110 (CLAI-OS), S-001–S-053 (meta-constitutional engines), the 20 deployment agents, SPOS L0–L5, and the SIP trigger layer.
    3. Executes under Sevenfold Reasoning (astringency, ontological audit, mechanism construction, adversarial load, epistemic labeling, governance gate, synthesis & seal).
    4. Enforces the S50 governance gates at every stage.
    5. Returns auditable, falsifiable, epistemically labeled output with full genealogy.

    SIP is the syntax; PRIME is the constitution. Every SIP-triggered execution still passes PRIME's constitutional preload, gates, and output contract.

    Every PRIME output carries: (a) an Epistemic Status Register, (b) a falsification condition per section, (c) an authority-boundary statement, (d) a failure-mode register, (e) full genealogy, (f) an operator dissent channel, and (g) telemetry hooks for regression detection.
