    Publications — The SAVANT Zenodo Corpus¶

    Author: Dr. Christabel Odeta — SAVANT / CLAI-OS / AEGIS-GLOBAL · Version 1.0 · October 2026 Classification: C0 INFORMATIONAL — nothing in this corpus confers clinical, legal, financial, or safety authority.

    This section publishes all twenty-two works of the SAVANT programme as deposited on Zenodo, each open access under CC-BY-4.0, each with its own page stating the work's abstract, its merit, and guidance for use. Every work is available as a PDF on this site and as a versioned, DOI-citable deposit on Zenodo.

    The series and how they fit together¶

    The corpus is organized as foundation → composition → evidence.

    • The Constitutional Intelligence Series (Papers 1–12) is the formal substrate. Papers 1–2 lay the foundation: an algebra of machine authority (granting, contraction, suspension, revocation, recovery) and a composition algebra that turns a collection of protocols into one governed system. Papers 3–5 build the epistemic machinery on top: what the system does not know, how it audits itself with operational stopping rules, and how executable authority is coupled to evidence quality. Papers 6–10 carry the structure into clinical and generative territory: objective-state transitions, survival under infrastructure failure, decision-making under resource constraint, governed protocol generation, and five domain modules. Papers 11–12 close the loop: an adversarial benchmark built to break the architecture, and an executable constitution with a reference runtime and a conformance discipline.
    • The Research Blueprint Series (Nº 1–3) turns the machinery outward to institutions: a conserved-quantity science of legitimacy, deterministic-first clinical intelligence as sovereign infrastructure, and blind-scored early warning of constitutional capture — each stated as a pre-registered empirical programme with explicit kill criteria.
    • Evidence Annex I is the evidence layer: the first executed results of the programme. A model check of the Paper 1 algebra (twelve properties verified; the strict form of Contraction Law 2 falsified and honored), the B-21 audit benchmark executed (16,000 trials; the recursion bound conditionally supported), and the Registered Frontier real-data test executed exactly as pre-registered (H1 rejected; kill criteria K1/K2 fired and honored). The annex publishes the corpus's refutations alongside its verifications, with code, seeds, and SHA-256 reproducibility hashes.

    Two further works — Paper 0 (the formal kernel) and Paper 13 (the claim architecture) — are reserved and withheld pending IP review; their abstracts are catalogued in the Constitutional Intelligence Series section. Their absence is stated rather than concealed, per the corpus's honest-absence doctrine.

    Reading paths¶

    • Evaluating the corpus's integrity: start with Evidence Annex I, then Paper 1 and Blueprint Nº 3.
    • Engineering a governed runtime: Papers 1, 5, and 12, with the configuration result in Evidence Annex I.
    • Clinical deployment: Papers 6, 7, 8, 10, then Blueprint Nº 2.
    • Civilian protection (AEGIS): start with the AEGIS-NG Master Document, generalize with AEGIS-GLOBAL, then the build path: Hardware Bundle → Manufacturing & Sourcing → Certification Curriculum, all anchored by Defensive Publication v5.0.
    • Institutional governance: Blueprint Nº 1, Blueprint Nº 3, and the real-data outcome in Evidence Annex I.
