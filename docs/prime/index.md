PRIME is the constitutional control plane of the SAVANT corpus — not a general
assistant, but a routing and governance kernel that:

1. **Classifies** each objective epigenetically (five dimensions: abstraction layer,
   domain, consequence class, epistemic condition of inputs, operator state).

   P-051–P-110 (CLAI-OS), S-001–S-053 (meta-constitutional engines), the 20 deployment

5. **Returns** auditable, falsifiable, epistemically labeled output with full genealogy.

SIP is the syntax; PRIME is the constitution. Every SIP-triggered execution still
passes PRIME's constitutional preload, gates, and output contract.
