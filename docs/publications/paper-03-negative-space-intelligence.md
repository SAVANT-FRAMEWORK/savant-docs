# Negative-Space Intelligence: The Failure-Boundary Calculus: Designing Intelligent Systems from Absence Inward (SAVANT Constitutional Intelligence Series, Paper 3)

**Author:** Dr. Christabel Odeta — SAVANT / CLAI-OS / AEGIS-GLOBAL · **Version 1.0 · October 2026**
**Classification:** C0 INFORMATIONAL — confers no clinical, legal, financial, or safety authority.

DOI: [10.5281/zenodo.23301055](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23301055) · License: CC-BY-4.0 · [Full text (PDF)](../constitutional-intelligence/assets/SAVANT-White-Paper-03-Negative-Space-Intelligence-v1.0.pdf)

## Abstract

A formal treatment of what a system does not know: assumption semantics, the absence lattice, detector design, the decay of ignorance, composition of missing assumptions, the silence calculus, and failure-boundary design as an engineering discipline.

## Merit

Most safety work catalogs what a system does; this paper formalizes what it does not know. Assumption semantics, the absence lattice, detector design, the decay of ignorance over time, the composition of missing assumptions, and a silence calculus for the cases where the most informative signal is the one that did not arrive.

For the safety engineer, the merit is disciplinary: failure-boundary design becomes an engineering practice with named objects and composition rules, rather than a postmortem genre. For the researcher, the absence lattice gives ignorance an algebraic structure that can be reasoned about and, crucially, monitored — bias detection in Paper 4 is built as a monitored absence, not as a sentiment. For the policymaker, the paper supplies language for a requirement most regimes need and few can state: that a system must declare what it cannot see, and must degrade its authority as that unseen region grows.

## Guidance for Use

**Who should read this work**

- Safety engineers designing detectors, monitors, and failure boundaries.
- Researchers working on uncertainty representation and unknown-unknowns.
- Policymakers writing declared-ignorance requirements into procurement or regulation.

**What it enables**

- A typed treatment of absence: missing assumptions are objects that compose, decay, and can be instrumented.
- The negative-space substrate for the monitored absences of Paper 4 and the evidence-decay dynamics of Paper 5.
- Failure-boundary design as a repeatable discipline rather than an accident report.
