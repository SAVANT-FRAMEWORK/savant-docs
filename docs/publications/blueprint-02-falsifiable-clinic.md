## Abstract

Deterministic-first clinical intelligence as sovereign infrastructure: from the OCCR technical specification to a constitutional-grade conformance class, an equity-parity financing covenant, and a manufacturing corridor for the majority world. Five contributions — the CGCA conformance class, equity parity as covenant, the sovereign manufacturing corridor, falsifiability-as-regulation, and the majority-world-first inversion — stated as a pre-registered empirical programme with explicit kill criteria. ## Merit

The clinical papers of the Constitutional Intelligence Series specify governed clinical intelligence; this blueprint proposes building it as sovereign infrastructure, majority-world first. Five contributions — the constitutional-grade CGCA conformance class, equity parity as covenant, the sovereign manufacturing corridor, falsifiability-as-regulation, and the majority-world-first inversion — are stated as a pre-registered empirical programme with explicit kill criteria.

For the health policymaker, falsifiability-as-regulation is the sharpest instrument: it inverts the usual audit posture by requiring that a deployed system be refutable in principle, not merely reviewed in practice. For majority-world governments and funders, the majority-world-first inversion is structural rather than rhetorical — the deployment corridor is designed for the constraint environments of Papers 7 and 8, not adapted to them afterward. As with the other blueprints, the public edition omits the internal commissioning, vehicle, intellectual-property, and funding apparatus; the instrument, its hypotheses, and its kill criteria are complete.
