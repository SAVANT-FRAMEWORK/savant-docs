This section publishes all twenty-two works of the SAVANT programme as deposited on

## The series and how they fit together

The corpus is organized as **foundation → composition → evidence**.

- **The Constitutional Intelligence Series (Papers 1–12)** is the formal substrate.
  Papers 1–2 lay the foundation: an algebra of machine authority (granting,
  contraction, suspension, revocation, recovery) and a composition algebra that
  turns a collection of protocols into one governed system. Papers 3–5 build the
  epistemic machinery on top: what the system does not know, how it audits itself
  with operational stopping rules, and how executable authority is coupled to
  evidence quality. Papers 6–10 carry the structure into clinical and generative
  territory: objective-state transitions, survival under infrastructure failure,
  decision-making under resource constraint, governed protocol generation, and five
  domain modules. Papers 11–12 close the loop: an adversarial benchmark built to
  break the architecture, and an executable constitution with a reference runtime
  and a conformance discipline.
- **The Research Blueprint Series (Nº 1–3)** turns the machinery outward to
  institutions: a conserved-quantity science of legitimacy, deterministic-first
  clinical intelligence as sovereign infrastructure, and blind-scored early warning
  of constitutional capture — each stated as a pre-registered empirical programme
  with explicit kill criteria.
- **Evidence Annex I** is the evidence layer: the first executed results of the
  programme. A model check of the Paper 1 algebra (twelve properties verified; the
  strict form of Contraction Law 2 falsified and honored), the B-21 audit benchmark
  executed (16,000 trials; the recursion bound conditionally supported), and the
  Registered Frontier real-data test executed exactly as pre-registered (H1
  rejected; kill criteria K1/K2 fired and honored). The annex publishes the
  corpus's refutations alongside its verifications, with code, seeds, and SHA-256
  reproducibility hashes.

Two further works — **Paper 0 (the formal kernel) and Paper 13 (the claim
architecture)** — are reserved and withheld pending IP review; their abstracts are
catalogued in the [Constitutional Intelligence Series](../constitutional-intelligence/index.md)
section. Their absence is stated rather than concealed, per the corpus's
honest-absence doctrine.
