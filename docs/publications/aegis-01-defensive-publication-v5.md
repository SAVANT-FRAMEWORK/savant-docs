This defensive publication establishes timestamped, citable prior art for fourteen patent families of the SAVANT FRAMEWORK ecosystem: AEGIS-P1–P8 (sovereign LoRa mesh architecture, passive child-safety beacon, vehicle-mounted hostile detection, multi-sensor threat fusion, adaptive anti-jam frequency hopping, sentinel multi-modal fusion node, federated learning with CRDT consistency, blockchain audit trail for civilian protection); CLAI-OS PPA-001–006 (epistemic lineage, epistemic consensus, analogical transfer, adaptive token budgeting, federated clinical audit ledger, jurisdiction compiler); and PPA-007, the cross-domain mesh–clinical integration protocol with cryptographic domain separation. ## Merit

This is the corpus's intellectual-property keystone: a single timestamped instrument that places all fourteen patent families into the public prior-art record, so that no third party can subsequently claim them. Its merit is structural rather than rhetorical — each family is disclosed at the level a patent examiner needs (system, method, apparatus; algorithms, protocols, falsifiability criteria), which is the standard that actually defeats later filings. The two domains it spans are kept strictly separate by design: AEGIS is the civilian-protection mesh; CLAI-OS is the clinical AI system; PPA-007 is their only point of contact, and it exists precisely to police that boundary with cryptographic domain separation.
