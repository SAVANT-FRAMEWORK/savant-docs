## Abstract

AEGIS-GLOBAL v1.0 elevates the AEGIS-NG v2.0 INFINITE civilian-protection architecture into a universal sovereign system: an open-architecture sovereignty transfer — blueprint, not product — adaptable to any nation-state where kidnapping, human trafficking, or organized abduction threatens civilians. The document specifies the universal core architecture of 24 node types across 7 layers with 8 regional adaptation deltas; a global threat taxonomy (T1–T6); jurisdictional compliance architectures and frequency plans for the United States, European Union/France, and Nigeria; the S50–S53 governance stack including Axiom A8 (Jurisdictional Sovereignty: sovereign keys, no data export, no vendor lock-in); a sovereign manufacturing blueprint with regional BOM adaptation; and a five-level training and certification curriculum (AEGIS-CL). All performance and cost figures are unverified engineering targets. Constitutional axioms A1–A8 are non-negotiable in every jurisdiction. Prior-art anchor: Defensive Publication v5.0.

## Merit

This is the generalization step: the Nigeria-proven architecture abstracted into a universal sovereign system, with the transfer model stated in one phrase — blueprint, not product. Its merit is the sovereignty engineering: Axiom A8 (sovereign keys, no data export, no vendor lock-in) is not a policy promise but an architectural property, and the jurisdictional compliance architectures show the system adapting to US, EU/France, and Nigeria regulatory regimes without diluting the constitutional axioms. The canon is fixed and public: 24 universal node types across 7 layers, 8 regional adaptation deltas, threat taxonomy T1–T6, governance stack S50–S53.
