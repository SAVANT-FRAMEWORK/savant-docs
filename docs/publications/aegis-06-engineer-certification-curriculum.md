# AEGIS-GLOBAL Local Engineer Certification Curriculum v1.0 — Capacity-Transfer Training for the AEGIS Node Family (SAVANT AEGIS Series)

**Author:** Dr. Christabel Odeta — SAVANT / CLAI-OS / AEGIS-GLOBAL · **Version 1.0 · October 2026**

## Abstract

This curriculum is the certification program through which local engineers gain complete, architect-independent competence over the AEGIS node family — assembly, firmware flashing and key provisioning, field deployment, maintenance and repair, and quality-control verification — across five modules with practical competence gates and no written examination. It is delivered in six languages (English, Hausa, Fulfulde, Igbo, Yoruba, Efik) with pictographic station cards and oral-first instruction so that literacy is not an entry barrier, while assessment gates are never lowered. The commercial frame is stated openly: certification training at $975 per engineer is the architect's only revenue line — no hardware margins, royalties, or platform fees — so that revenue exists precisely when dependency ends. The document includes falsifiability tests for the capacity-transfer claim, the revenue-exclusivity claim, and the low-literacy adaptation. Companion documents: Manufacturing Specification and node hardware specifications.

## Merit

This is the document that makes the transfer claim social. Its merit is the deliberate inversion of the dependency model: the architect's only revenue line ($975 per engineer certification) exists precisely when dependency ends, and the curriculum says so in writing, with a falsifiability test attached. The low-literacy adaptation is engineered rather than aspirational — six languages, pictographic station cards, oral-first instruction, practical competence gates, no written examination — while the assessment standard is explicitly never lowered. Architect-independent competence is the stated success criterion, which is the strongest form a capacity-transfer claim can take.
