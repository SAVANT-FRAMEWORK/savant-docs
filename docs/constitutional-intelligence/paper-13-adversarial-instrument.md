# The Adversarial Instrument: The SAVANT Benchmark for Machine Authority — White Paper 11

**Version 1.0 · 2026-10 · C0 INFORMATIONAL — mathematical specification; all properties stated as proof obligations until discharged.**

The program's deferred instruction, discharged: a benchmark built not to flatter the architecture but to break it. Twelve adversarial scenario classes (SC-1…SC-12) attacking every load-bearing mechanism of the Papers 0–10 kernel — authority acquisition, overreach, uncertainty, objective drift, conflicting instructions, missing evidence, infrastructure collapse, resource scarcity, irreversible actions, human disagreement, stale knowledge, recovery after failure. Nine metrics with formal estimators. A five-arm ablation ladder from unconstrained frontier model to full constitutional runtime. And the verdict rule as law: the verdict is a profile, never a scalar (invariant I-NOSCORE); every failure is logged as a counterexample addressed to the specific mechanism that failed and enters the architecture's revision process — never its marketing. Closes with falsifiable hypotheses H85–H91, benchmarks B-48–B-50, and a reproducibility constitution.
