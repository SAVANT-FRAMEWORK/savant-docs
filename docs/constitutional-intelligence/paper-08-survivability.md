# Survivability: Continuity of Intelligence Under Infrastructure Failure — White Paper Nº 8

**Version 1.0 · 2026-10 · C0 INFORMATIONAL — mathematical specification; all properties stated as proof obligations until discharged.**

The mathematical foundation for the Survivability Engine: the degradation lattice L0–L4 (cloud, local, edge, paper, human) as a chain of capability envelopes, descent and ascent predicates, the safety-equivalence invariant that defines graceful degradation as losing capability without losing constitutional safety, deferred obligations and the recovery log, and survivability as a design discipline rather than a hope. Descent is deterministic; re-ascent is gated by an integrity validator.

## Falsifiability Test

This abstract fails if graceful degradation is cited as preserving full capability, or if re-ascent is represented as automatic rather than validator-gated.
