# Constitutional Intelligence Series — Published White Papers

**Source Architecture:** Dr. Christabel Odeta — SAVANT FRAMEWORK · CLAI-OS · AEGIS-GLOBAL
**Version:** 1.0 · 2026-10
**Classification:** C0 INFORMATIONAL — design and mathematical specifications. Nothing in this series confers clinical, legal, financial, or safety authority.

The Constitutional Intelligence Series is the formal substrate beneath the SAVANT
corpus: a research program of fourteen works — **twelve published papers (Papers 1–12)
plus two reserved works (Paper 0 and Paper 13), withheld pending IP review** — that
specifies **bounded machine authority** as mathematics: definitions, axioms, an
authority algebra, invariants, transition rules, conflict operators, composition laws,
a generative lifecycle, a measurement instrument, and an executable realization —
with every named property stated as a **proof obligation**, not a theorem, and every
load-bearing claim carrying an explicit epistemic status.

| Tag | Meaning |
|-----|---------|

word "proven" is reserved for discharged proof obligations.
