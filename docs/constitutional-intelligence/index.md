The Constitutional Intelligence Series is the formal substrate beneath the SAVANT
corpus: an eleven-chunk research program that specifies **bounded machine authority**
as mathematics — definitions, axioms, an authority algebra, invariants, transition
rules, conflict operators, composition laws, and a generative lifecycle — with every
named property stated as a **proof obligation**, not a theorem, and every load-bearing
claim carrying an explicit epistemic status.
