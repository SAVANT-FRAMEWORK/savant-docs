# Constitutional Intelligence Series — Published White Papers

**Source Architecture:** Dr. Christabel Odeta — SAVANT FRAMEWORK · CLAI-OS · AEGIS-GLOBAL
**Version:** 1.0 · 2026-10
**Classification:** C0 INFORMATIONAL — design and mathematical specifications. Nothing in this series confers clinical, legal, financial, or safety authority.

The Constitutional Intelligence Series is the formal substrate beneath the SAVANT
corpus: a fourteen-paper research program — an eleven-paper formal kernel plus three
consolidation works — that specifies **bounded machine authority** as mathematics:
definitions, axioms, an authority algebra, invariants, transition rules, conflict
operators, composition laws, a generative lifecycle, a measurement instrument, and an
executable realization — with every named property stated as a **proof obligation**,
not a theorem, and every load-bearing claim carrying an explicit epistemic status.
