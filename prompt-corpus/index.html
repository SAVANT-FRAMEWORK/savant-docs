    The Canon¶

    The SAVANT FRAMEWORK prompt corpus is a governed canon of 75 prompts across 15 tiers. Every prompt carries an ID, version, content hash, and parent hash; genealogy is governed by the constitutional canon. The corpus is the substrate beneath every system documented on this site — SPOS executes it, SIP invokes it, S50-ABSOLUTE governs it, and CLAI-OS (P51–P70) is its clinical expression.

    Honest absence: PENDING entries are disclosed as ledger rows only. There are no placeholders, no reconstructions, no aspirational stubs. A prompt either exists in canonical form (PRESENT) or it does not (PENDING).

    License Gate¶

    The corpus is dual-licensed at a hard boundary:

    • KEY-001 – KEY-020 (Tiers 1–4: Foundation, RAG, Agents, Production) — AGPL-3.0. Open, forkable, auditable.
    • KEY-021 – KEY-075 (Tiers 5–15) — Savant-Commercial-1.0. Commercial licensing terms: inquiries via the GitHub organization.

    The CLAI-OS clinical series (P51–P70) documented under CLAI-OS Operations is AGPL-3.0 for Global South, non-profit, and academic deployment, with commercial stewardship for for-profit use.
